# Bolero, romantic ballad, rock en español and contemporary Latin pop: artist and fusion case-study prompts

**Interpretation:** experimental production exercises rather than claims about exact songs. **Region:** Mexico, Caribbean and Ibero-American pop scenes.

## Historical starting point
Bolero's slow pulse, guitar/piano arpeggios and dramatic vocal cadence must be differentiated from Cuban bolero-chacha and generic ballad. Contemporary pop and rock en español are larger families requiring explicit subgenre naming.

## Contemporary transformation
Write a vocal phrase with long cadence/space, harmonic tension at key words, melodic instrumental answers, and small dynamic increases. Latin pop may adopt other grooves but shouldn't use 'Latin' as a self-sufficient rhythm label.

## Evidence discipline
Seek original recordings, interviews, credited personnel, ethnomusicology, and first-party historical archives before claiming track-level notes, tempos or musician credits. Avoid extrapolating every performer into a 'genre default'.

## Tests
1. Remove the traditional instrument/role and see if the arrangement identity collapses.
2. Compare contemporary overlay active/inactive without changing the melody.
3. Verify spec schema, and that no two parallel layers claim the same dimension.
