# Funk carioca, tamborzão, funk melody and Brazilian funk-pop: artist and fusion case-study prompts

**Interpretation:** experimental production exercises rather than claims about exact songs. **Region:** Brazil.

## Historical starting point
Electronic programmed beats and bass shape the identity; specify rhythmic subdivision and local variant (funk carioca, tamborzão, mandelão) rather than assigning a generic reggaeton dembow.

## Contemporary transformation
Work from kick, synthetic percussion, sub-bass and vocal rhythm. Use meaningful beat-mutes, pitched one-shots, chopped vocal rhythm and sectional synth changes. Make room for Portuguese vocal prosody, not only dancefloor looping.

## Evidence discipline
Seek original recordings, interviews, credited personnel, ethnomusicology, and first-party historical archives before claiming track-level notes, tempos or musician credits. Avoid extrapolating every performer into a 'genre default'.

## Tests

3. Verify spec schema, and that no two parallel layers claim the same dimension.
