# Regional mexicano: ranchera, mariachi, norteño, banda and sierreño: artist innovation and fusion study

This is a **compositional analysis guide**, not a transcription, authenticated quotation or voice-cloning instruction. Do not infer track-specific tempos, keys, clave orientations, MIDI notes or recording credits without checking an actual source recording/score or credit.

## Tradition to present
**Geographic lineage:** Mexico and Mexican diaspora.
**Branches worth comparing:** ranchera, mariachi, huapango, norteño, banda sinaloense, sierreño, norteño sax, grupero.
**Root grammar:** Differentiate 3/4 ranchera, 6/8 huapango, polka-like norteño and banda marches; no universal 'Mexican beat'. Identify meter and 6/8 vs 3/4 hemiola before writing.
**Contemporary production axis:** Preserve ensemble differentiation: tuba/accordion/strings are not interchangeable MIDI placeholders.

## Fusion design exercises
