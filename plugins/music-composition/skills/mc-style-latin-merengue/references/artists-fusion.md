# Merengue típico, orchestra, merengue-house and urbano: artist innovation and fusion study

This is a **compositional analysis guide**, not a transcription, authenticated quotation or voice-cloning instruction. Do not infer track-specific tempos, keys, clave orientations, MIDI notes or recording credits without checking an actual source recording/score or credit.

## Tradition to present
**Geographic lineage:** Dominican Republic.
**Branches worth comparing:** merengue típico/perico ripiao, orquestal, merengue-house, merengue de calle, merengue-pop.
**Root grammar:** Tambora two-sided gestures and güira continuous subdivisions are independent from an accordion/sax riff. Specify whether the reference counts in 2 or 4 and distinguish double-time notation.
**Contemporary production axis:** Keep güira in a separate high transient slot from hats; subtract horns when the vocal melody needs emphasis.
