# Vallenato paseo, son, merengue vallenato, puya and modern Colombian pop-vallenato: artist and fusion case-study prompts

**Interpretation:** experimental production exercises rather than claims about exact songs. **Region:** Colombia / Caribbean region.

## Historical starting point
Vallenato has accordion, caja and guacharaca interplay; 'merengue' here is a vallenato air, not automatically Dominican merengue. Specify the air before selecting the bass pulse.

## Contemporary transformation
Accordion answers lead/voice; caja and guacharaca hold different beat subdivisions; the paseo is a distinct expressive groove. Pop-vallenato keeps characteristic accordion phrases while bass, drums and guitar may expand the palette.

## Evidence discipline
Seek original recordings, interviews, credited personnel, ethnomusicology, and first-party historical archives before claiming track-level notes, tempos or musician credits. Avoid extrapolating every performer into a 'genre default'.

## Tests
1. Remove the traditional instrument/role and see if the arrangement identity collapses.
2. Compare contemporary overlay active/inactive without changing the melody.
3. Verify spec schema, and that no two parallel layers claim the same dimension.
