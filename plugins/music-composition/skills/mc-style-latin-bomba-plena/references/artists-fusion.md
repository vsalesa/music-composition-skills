# Afro-Puerto Rican bomba, plena and contemporary Puerto Rican roots fusion: artist and fusion case-study prompts

**Interpretation:** experimental production exercises rather than claims about exact songs. **Region:** Puerto Rico.

## Historical starting point
Bomba's barrel drummers respond to the dancer's movements in a dialogic format; plena employs panderetas and güiro. Neither should be assigned a mandatory Afro-Cuban son clave. Distinguish each subfamily.

## Contemporary transformation
Bomba: design a dancer/lead-drum call and response, with rhythmic rules tied to interaction rather than a fixed four-bar loop. Plena: design layered pandereta pulse and lead/chorus. Contemporary arrangement can place them in distinct song sections with urban or jazz elements.

## Evidence discipline
Seek original recordings, interviews, credited personnel, ethnomusicology, and first-party historical archives before claiming track-level notes, tempos or musician credits. Avoid extrapolating every performer into a 'genre default'.

## Tests
1. Remove the traditional instrument/role and see if the arrangement identity collapses.
2. Compare contemporary overlay active/inactive without changing the melody.
3. Verify spec schema, and that no two parallel layers claim the same dimension.
