# Northeastern Brazilian baião, xote, forró, forró eletrônico and piseiro: artist and fusion case-study prompts

**Interpretation:** experimental production exercises rather than claims about exact songs. **Region:** Brazil / Northeast.

## Historical starting point
Zabumba (two surfaces), triangle pulse and accordion phrasing are the reference ensemble; baião and xote use different accents and tempo feel. Not every forró uses a 'Brazilian clave'.

## Contemporary transformation
Compose an accordion melodic answer, distinct triangle subdivision, percussive zabumba low-end and dancing bass. Piseiro shifts toward a keyboard-led, repetitive modern sound and shorter hook structures; choose whether the accordion remains or hands the hook to keyboard.

## Evidence discipline
Seek original recordings, interviews, credited personnel, ethnomusicology, and first-party historical archives before claiming track-level notes, tempos or musician credits. Avoid extrapolating every performer into a 'genre default'.

## Tests
1. Remove the traditional instrument/role and see if the arrangement identity collapses.
2. Compare contemporary overlay active/inactive without changing the melody.
3. Verify spec schema, and that no two parallel layers claim the same dimension.
