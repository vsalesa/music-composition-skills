# Andean music, huayno, festejo, landó and modern Andean/Afro-Peruvian crossovers: artist and fusion case-study prompts

**Interpretation:** experimental production exercises rather than claims about exact songs. **Region:** Peru, Bolivia, Ecuador and Andean diaspora.

## Historical starting point
Huayno melodic and rhythmic systems differ from Afro-Peruvian festejo/landó; these should not be combined into an undifferentiated Peruvian template. Landó and festejo have distinct cajón and bass interplay.

## Contemporary transformation
For Andean music, assign charango, quena/zampoña or violin/harp by region; for Afro-Peruvian, center cajón, guitar and contrapuntal bass; choose tempo, meter and regional variant from evidence.

## Evidence discipline
Seek original recordings, interviews, credited personnel, ethnomusicology, and first-party historical archives before claiming track-level notes, tempos or musician credits. Avoid extrapolating every performer into a 'genre default'.

## Tests
1. Remove the traditional instrument/role and see if the arrangement identity collapses.
2. Compare contemporary overlay active/inactive without changing the melody.
3. Verify spec schema, and that no two parallel layers claim the same dimension.
