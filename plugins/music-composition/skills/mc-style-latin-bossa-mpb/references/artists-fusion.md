# Bossa nova, MPB, tropicalismo and contemporary Brazilian pop: artist and fusion case-study prompts

**Interpretation:** experimental production exercises rather than claims about exact songs. **Region:** Brazil.

## Historical starting point
Bossa draws on samba's rhythmic sensibility at reduced dynamics and intimate placement; nylon-string guitar is an active counter-rhythm, not merely a chord pad. Avoid mechanically alternating chord changes on odd/even bars as a universal requirement.

## Contemporary transformation
Use intimate guitar/vocal independence, extended chords and bass landing clearly against guitar anticipations. MPB ranges broadly across regional styles, rock, orchestration and electronic production: specify the particular lineage, never assume all MPB is bossa.

## Evidence discipline
Seek original recordings, interviews, credited personnel, ethnomusicology, and first-party historical archives before claiming track-level notes, tempos or musician credits. Avoid extrapolating every performer into a 'genre default'.

## Tests
1. Remove the traditional instrument/role and see if the arrangement identity collapses.
2. Compare contemporary overlay active/inactive without changing the melody.
3. Verify spec schema, and that no two parallel layers claim the same dimension.
